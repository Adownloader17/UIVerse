## Feedback Form for Frontend Users

We've added a user-friendly feedback form to help us understand your needs better and improve our resources!

### Features:
- 📝 Easy-to-use feedback submission
- ⭐ Rate your experience (1-5 stars)
- 📋 Multiple feedback categories (General, Content, Tools, Bugs, Features)
- 💌 Option to subscribe to updates
- 🎨 Modern, responsive design
- ✅ Form validation for better user experience

### How to Use:
Simply open `feedback.html` in your web browser to access the feedback form. Fill out the required fields and share your thoughts with us!

### What's Included:
- `feedback.html` - The feedback form page
- `styles.css` - Beautiful, modern styling
- `script.js` - Form handling and validation logic
